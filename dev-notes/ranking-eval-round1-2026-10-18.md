# Ranking experiments, round 1: realistic intent, the work neighbourhood, milestones, hold-out PPR tuning (2026-10-18)

James, 2026-10-18: *"keep testing and experimenting to most of low or negatives go away ... let's just make sure we get closer to the shape"*;
*"intent comes from people's purpose to create ... sometimes it's a general idea made up of little milestones and ideas"*.

Harness: `engine/examples/rank_eval.rs --round1 on [--pre glia|kina] --tune on --pick base,d85`. Same dataset, metrics, paired
commit-cluster bootstrap and controls as the first run; every first-run row reproduces exactly on glia / Kina / quokka-stack.
Private repos (Kina, quokka-stack, Engram, turps, slurpngulp): aggregate metrics only.

## Bottom line

1. **Realistic intent keeps most of T1's gain.** The subject line alone (H9s50 = 0.5 mm(BM25 on the subject) + 0.5 mm(P1)) keeps
   89-106% of the optimistic H9a50's gain over P1 on 6 repos, 71% on Kina. glia's packet title + rationale (T1pre, truly
   pre-change) keeps 93%, the same as the subject. Kina's task rows keep 20%: the row text is a poor BM25 query against
   commit messages. On flutter/samples every text signal hurts (H9a50 is 0.075 below P1).
2. **The work neighbourhood carries intent without text, but only on sequential histories.**
   - W5 (the last 5 commits' files) beats its random-window control on 6 of 9 repos. M (the current milestone's files) beats
     its shuffled control on 5.
   - Blended into P1, W5 recovers 60-187% of the text gain on 5 of 7 untuned repos: shell, websocket, Engram, turps, slurpngulp.
   - It is at chance on glia: no test commit shares a code file with the commit before it, because the wave scheduler keeps
     concurrent packets file-disjoint. It hurts on flutter/samples (-0.131).
   - Once text is present, it adds nothing except on Engram (+0.023).
3. **The hybrids picked on glia + Kina generalise.**
   - H18s[PT*] (mm subject BM25 + mm co-change + mm tuned PPR, each 1/3) beats P1 on all 7 untuned repos, significantly on 5.
   - Choosing per repo online among fixed hybrids makes the negatives go away. At each test commit, SELt uses the candidate
     with the best recall@10 over the earlier test commits, so it is leak-free.
   - SELt is above P1 on all 9 repos (CI above 0 on 8) and at or above the first run's optimistic H9a50 on 8 of 9
     (significantly on flutter/samples, +0.142, and turps, +0.086). It is 0.024 below on Kina.
4. **Tuned PPR earns a little.**
   - The pick from glia + Kina is base weights, damping 0.85, sum pooling. It beats P1 on 6 of 7 unseen repos (+0.007 to +0.034
     recall@10; MRR up to +0.23).
   - Against BFS it is mixed on unseen repos: 3 wins (samples +0.127, turps +0.106, Engram +0.024), 2 losses (shell -0.073,
     slurpngulp -0.034), 2 not significant.
   - Edge weights are not a lever: the retuned sets move recall@10 by at most 0.004 on the tuning repos.
